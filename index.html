Hello, I'm Sofia Lumière — your wedding day storyteller.

I believe every wedding holds a universe of quiet glances, joyful tears, and breathtaking beauty that deserves to be preserved with intention. My role is to disappear into your day and surface with images that carry the full weight of the love you felt.

With over a decade of experience photographing weddings across Southeast Asia, I blend fine-art aesthetics with an instinctive documentary eye — creating portraits that feel like paintings and candids that stop time.
